# Research protocol and decision log

## Evidence ledger

Write short, atomic claims. Add every consulted source to the claim, including weak or conflicting sources. Mark a source as `independent` only when the named publisher performed its own reporting or analysis; copies of AP, DVIDS, a press release, a partner article, a mirror, or a quoted version of the first item are not independent corroboration.

## Grade rubric

| Grade | Meaning | Final fact body? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| S | Two or more authoritative records plus two distinct independent reports | Yes |
| A | Official/primary record plus a genuine independent second publisher | Yes |
| B | Clearly attributed non-core claim with authoritative evidence | Yes, attributed |
| C | Incomplete lead or missing a required check | No |
| D | Out of scope, unsupported, or unresolved conflict | No |

An unresolved conflict automatically produces D for a core claim. Keep it in the audit section with both source URLs. A reviewer, not the tool, can later mark a conflict resolved with a documented basis.

## Photo record

Each image must retain the binary asset URL and the original record page. The record must contain the complete caption, creator, and date. Store one or more URLs that directly identify the person shown, and a distinct page or statement that supports the copyright/licence status. A military asset page may support both only where it actually contains both statements; do not infer public-domain status from a `.mil` host.

Photos are useful only once every required field is present. Five to ten verified photographs is a planning target, not an acceptance criterion. Report the shortfall.
